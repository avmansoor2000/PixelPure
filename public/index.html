FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does the watermark remover work?

You paint over the watermark or object. The selection is sent with your image to an AI inpainting model, which generates what should be behind it. The result is blended back so every pixel outside your selection is unchanged.

Which file formats are supported?

JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP up to 15 MB each. You can download results as lossless PNG or high-quality JPG.

What does 2× and 4× enhancement do?

The image is upscaled by an AI super-resolution model that reconstructs fine detail, with optional noise reduction and an automatic light and clarity pass.

Is it legal to remove watermarks?

Only remove watermarks from images you own or have permission to edit — for example, your own photos, licensed images or old drafts. Removing a watermark to use someone else's copyrighted work without permission may be illegal and is against our Terms.

What happens to my images?

Images are stored temporarily for processing and permanently deleted after 60 minutes. EXIF and location metadata are stripped on upload. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I process several images at once?

Yes. Select or drop multiple files and switch between them in the editor's image tray. Each image keeps its own edit history.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The editor adapts to touch screens: paint with your finger, use the Pan tool to move around zoomed images, and download directly to your device.
