About PixelPure

Clean images, without the busywork.

PixelPure is an AI image editor focused on two jobs: removing what shouldn't be in a picture, and making what remains look its best. Brush over a watermark, date stamp, logo or stray object and our inpainting models rebuild the background. Upscale 2× or 4× when you need more resolution for print, product pages or presentations.

How we build

We integrate best-in-class AI image models behind a single, simple editor, and we only change the pixels you select — everything else stays exactly as it was. Uploads are validated, stripped of metadata and deleted automatically.

Responsible use

PixelPure is meant for images you own or are licensed to edit. Please respect photographers, artists and stock agencies — see our Terms.
