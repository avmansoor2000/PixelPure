Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you use PixelPure we process the images you upload, the selection masks you draw and basic technical data (IP address, browser type) needed to operate and protect the service, for example rate limiting.

How your images are processed

  • Uploads are validated and re-encoded on our servers. EXIF, GPS and other embedded metadata are removed.
  • To perform AI removal or enhancement, the image and mask are sent to our AI processing partner (such as Stability AI, Clipdrop or Replicate, depending on configuration) solely to produce your result.
  • Uploaded images and results are stored temporarily and deleted automatically after 60 minutes. You can also remove an image earlier from the editor's image tray.
  • We do not sell your images or use them to train models.

Cookies

The editor does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You may request access to or deletion of personal data we hold by contacting us. Because images are deleted automatically, we usually hold none beyond the retention window.
